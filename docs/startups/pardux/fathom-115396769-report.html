Highlights

  • Pardux successfully pivoted its business model from an e-commerce solution integrator to a logistics solution integrator, specifically for Next Day Delivery to small and medium-sized companies, including those in rural areas. This pivot resulted in 15,000 shipments and $180,000 in sales since the change. The company has achieved product-market fit, with a full sales agenda and a robust pipeline. They have capitalized on the trend of businesses moving online due to insecurity in Ecuador and the high demand for cash-on-delivery services. Pardux has secured a better contract with Arcuri, becoming a top client, and is in discussions with Servientrega to diversify logistics providers. Their sales process is highly efficient, with 20% of clients closing on the first call. They have developed proprietary software using AI to certify addresses, leading to a 96% delivery efficiency rate and a return rate of less than 5%. They also provide real-time delivery updates to customers via SMS. Pardux is exploring a potential merger with AVE Online, a Colombian company, to expand into a larger market.

Risks

  • Pardux faces a challenge with the closure of offers due to high demand. They also have a 15-day gap in receiving funds from couriers and dispersing them to clients, which causes dissatisfaction among customers. Traditional financing options like bank loans are not viable due to high interest rates. They are also dealing with issues related to theft and loss of packages, which they are actively working to resolve. The company needs to find ways to differentiate itself from competitors as clients grow and gain direct access to courier services.
