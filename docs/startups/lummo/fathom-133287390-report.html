Highlights

  • Lummo has developed an AI infrastructure that reduces the time for feasibility studies from 1-2 months to 2 minutes and patient recruitment from 6-8 months to 10 minutes. They have 8 pilot clients and are expanding their operations to include major hospitals in Colombia and potentially Mexico. They have a multi-annual contract model for pilot clients, starting at $600/month and scaling up to $49,000/year. They are raising a $1.5 million round to achieve 1.2 million ARR and expand their vision to become an AI-CRO.

Asks

  • Lummo is seeking to close a $1.5 million funding round at a $7.5 million post-money valuation. They are also looking for introductions to hospitals and CROs, particularly in Mexico (IMSS) and Colombia (Clínica Santa Fe). They are open to sharing their deck with potential investors.

Risks

  • The company is currently in a pilot phase and needs feedback to improve its algorithms. They are working on integrating with hospital databases and setting up clinical trials, which can take time. They also face the challenge of convincing CROs to sponsor hospitals and navigate different healthcare systems like IMSS in Mexico and CISBEN in Colombia.
