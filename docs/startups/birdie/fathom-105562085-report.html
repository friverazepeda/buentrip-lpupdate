Highlights

  • Birdie has developed an AI-powered platform that automates the extraction and processing of documents (PDFs, Excel, CCBs, images) for international trade, significantly reducing manual effort. The platform offers automated tracking of shipments, including real-time status updates from shipping lines and customs. A compliance engine identifies discrepancies in documents against customs laws, calculating potential fines and suggesting corrective actions. Birdie's architecture is headless, allowing clients to integrate its functionalities into their existing systems (e.g., SAP, Odoo, Google AppSheet) without needing to use Birdie's UI. The company has secured pilot programs with major importers like Coppel, the largest maritime importer in Mexico, with potential annual contracts exceeding $100,000. They have also formed a commercial partnership with AJR, a foreign trade company with 1,500 importer clients, to expand market reach. Birdie's focus is shifting towards demonstrating direct cost savings and avoidance of fines for clients, aiming to increase contract values based on the value generated.

Risks

  • The main challenge highlighted is the industry's reliance on Excel and the difficulty in getting clients to abandon it. Birdie addresses this by integrating with existing Excel workflows. Another challenge is the complexity and lack of standardization in documents from various suppliers, especially international ones. The company also notes the increasing regulatory scrutiny and potential for significant fines from customs authorities, which their platform aims to mitigate.
