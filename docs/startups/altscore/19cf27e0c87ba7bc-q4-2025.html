Summary

AltScore closed 2025 with a strong Q4, showing positive results from difficult decisions made earlier in the year to focus the company and maximize long-term growth. The company achieved sharper execution, a more concentrated product and GTM motion, greater focus, and improving financial efficiency while continuing to grow. Key achievements include institutionalizing stricter ICP guardrails, leading to higher ICP fit scores and qualified pipeline, and significant expansion and record usage with core customers, exceeding NDR targets. A new Capital-as-a-Service deployment went live with Andina Argentina and Banco Galicia. Challenges remain in proving faster and more repeatable enterprise sales under the new framework. Product development focused on packaging, sales enablement, and a GenAI agent to accelerate credit workflow design. The company reported $2.6M USD Total ARR EoQ, 18 months of runway, and 154K Total Unique Borrowers Enabled / Month. AltScore enters 2026 leaner, more focused, with stronger PMF signals, healthier growth dynamics, and a clearer path to scale.

Risks

  • Proving Speed and Repeatability Under Adjusted ICP and GTM Framework: The main challenge is to prove that new enterprise logos can be onboarded faster and more repeatably under the new ICP and GTM framework.
