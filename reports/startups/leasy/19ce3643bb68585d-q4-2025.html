Highlights

  • LEASY achieved a strong end to the year in Q4-25, signing 565 new contracts (+15.5% vs. Q3-25 and +64.2% vs. Q4-24) across Peru and Mexico, driven by Mexico's maturing operations. Peru hit new sales records with 382 contracts signed and 423 cars delivered. The Retail Channel in Peru launched, securing its first sale. Mexico saw record sales with 183 new contracts and 162 cars delivered, and is preparing for Monterrey's launch. DRYFT surpassed $1M in revenue in 2025 (+138% vs FY-24) and launched PitBox, a modular express car service solution. The tech team rolled out a new Sales Platform and Auto-Evaluation in Peru, and a Predictive Analytics module. The A-2 funding round closed, raising $6M in Equity, and the company is preparing for a Series B targeting $30-50M in Equity and $100-200M in debt.

Risks

  • The main challenge for DRYFT was avoiding sinking too much CAPEX and committing to an individual location, which was addressed by the PitBox solution. In Mexico, the process to become a SOFOM is complex and time-consuming (9-12 months). Optimizing sales channels and improving conversion rates remain a focus for Mexico in Q1-26.
