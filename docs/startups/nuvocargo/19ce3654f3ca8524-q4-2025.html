Highlights

  • Nuvocargo grew revenues from $17M to $37M (117% YoY) for FY 24 vs. 25, and grew gross profit from ~$1.4M to ~$4.4M (200% YoY). Margins nearly doubled YoY, TAM grew by 100x, burn is decreasing, and multiple exciting pilot conversations for multi-year SaaS/AI contracts were closed. P&Ls across all three business units (3PL, Customs, MTS) are now EBITDA positive. The company is under exclusivity for an acquisition that would take them to $50-$55M+ in annual revenue, ~$7.5-$8.5M gross profit, and generating $2M+ of adj. EBITDA overnight. AI implementation led to a 130% EBITDA per load increase and ~45% increase in gross margin per load for Merge, with a ~40% reduction in cost to serve per load. A COO, Jim Keating, was hired to run business units and grow EBITDA. R&D roadmap shifts to customer-facing control tower, with new engineers hired. Three net-new AI Agents are live in production, handling real customer workflows end-to-end and reducing 40% of the cost associated with operating a load. Autonomous Bid Negotiation and Awarding by AI agents resulted in 66% of shipments having AI-assisted bids and 28% of all loads being fully sourced, negotiated, and booked by AI. New partner integrations with Greenscreens and Mothership were launched.
