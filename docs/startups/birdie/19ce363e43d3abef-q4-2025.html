Highlights

  • Coppel pilot officially kicked off with 40+ users across compliance and logistics teams. BuenTrip Ventures joined Birdie as investors. Strategic partnership with AJR Consultores, Mexico’s largest international trade consulting firm, now offers Birdie as its recommended supply chain digitalization platform.
